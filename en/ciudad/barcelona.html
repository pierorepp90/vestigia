Beneath the Barri Gòtic lie two thousand years of layers: a Roman wall, the old Jewish quarter, and the scars of a civil war that can still be read in the stone.

Trails in Barcelona

Barri Gòtic

The Secret of the Gothic Quarter

Eight stops between the Cathedral and Plaça Sant Jaume to read what the stone of the Gothic Quarter has been telling for centuries: a Roman wall, a bridge that's not as medieval as it looks, and the marks of a 1938 bombing.

El Born · La Ribera

The Merchants of La Ribera

From the Arc de Triomf to the Gothic palaces of Carrer Montcada, through the district that financed its own basilica by hand and paid for it with the total destruction of its streets in 1714.

From Miró's mosaic at the Boqueria to the city's oldest bar, by way of Barcelona's oldest church and a Gaudí palace hidden in the former red-light district.
